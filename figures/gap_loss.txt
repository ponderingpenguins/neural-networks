Eight short runs of the MLP projector that change one setting at a time (seed 1 is the dense-checkpoint run clip1; s2/s3 = other seeds; lr 2e-4 and 5e-5; initial projector scale 0.5 and 2; batch size 2), all with gradient clipping. Each panel: training loss (blue), validation loss with the right audio (orange), validation loss with another utterance's audio (dashed orange) and the audio gap, the difference of the two (red, right axis, nats per token). The gap is about 0 on the plateau, so the model ignores the audio there, and it rises to 4-5 nats when the loss drops. The dotted line is the drop step (582, 631, 730, 473, 870, none within 1,000 steps for init 2, 660, 749). Each setting is a single run; clip1, s2 and s3 are three seeds of the same base setting.

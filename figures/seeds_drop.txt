Validation loss of the linear projector, the MLP projector and the MLP with norm penalty 0.1, three seeds each (1,500 steps, lr 1e-4, gradient clipping 1.0). Left: mean and standard deviation of the validation loss. All three groups sit on a plateau at about 3.5 and then fall to 0.4-0.5. Right: the step of the drop (first evaluation with validation loss below 2.5; points are seeds, bar is mean +- std): linear 600/900/900, MLP 700/700/800, MLP + penalty 0.1 800/800/800. The groups overlap, so the drop step does not separate the linear baseline from the MLP. With n = 3 the error bars are crude.

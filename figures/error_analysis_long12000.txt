Error analysis of the MLP projector trained for 12,000 steps (500 test.clean utterances, corpus WER 12.3%). Left: histogram of the per-utterance WER (values above 1 shown as 1); 26% of the utterances have no error, most of the rest have WER below 0.3. Right: utterance WER against reference length in words. The curved stripes are the discrete values of errors / length. Short utterances are noisy (one error in 3 words is 0.33) and the few utterances of 70 or more words with WER 0.7-1.0 are the worst cases. The same model has 9.2% substitutions, 1.8% insertions and 1.3% deletions.

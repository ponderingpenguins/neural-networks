Distribution of the cosine similarity of every projected frame to its single nearest token embedding, at initialisation, four checkpoints and the end of the 12,000-step MLP run. All curves lie between 0.10 and 0.19, peaking at about 0.14. The dashed line at about 0.62 is the same quantity for real text embeddings (nearest other token). The frames never come close to a vocabulary token; training changes the distribution only a little.

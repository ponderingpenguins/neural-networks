Cosine similarity between every projected speech frame and the LM's token embeddings, as histograms (log density) at initialisation, steps 3,000-12,000 and the end of the 12,000-step MLP run. Grey: random vocabulary tokens; red: tokens of the utterance's own transcript; dotted: cosines of unrelated random directions (sd 0.031). The transcript tokens are only slightly shifted to the right of the others (mean +0.015, sd 0.035, against 0.000, sd 0.031) and the shift hardly changes during training. The projected frames are close to unrelated to the spoken words' embeddings in direction, even though the model transcribes with 12% WER.
